# Cellpose Plus

## Overview
Cellpose Plus is an advanced framework aimed at performing precise and efficient cell segmentation and image analysis tasks in both two-dimensional (2D) and three-dimensional (3D) datasets. The architecture integrates deep learning methodologies to enhance the segmentation capabilities, providing robust solutions suited for complex cellular structures in microscopy images.

## Purpose
The primary objective of Cellpose Plus is to enable researchers and professionals in the life sciences to extract significant biological insights from intricate image data. This is achieved through a suite of functionalities that include:
- Command-line and graphical interfaces for intuitive interaction.
- Support for real-time image preprocessing, segmentation, and evaluation, catering to various input formats.
- Implementation of denoising and augmentation techniques to refine image quality prior to analysis.
- Utilities for efficient management of computational resources, optimizing performance for diverse model training and testing scenarios.

Cellpose Plus thus serves as a comprehensive toolkit, streamlining the segmentation workflow and enhancing the ability to derive meaningful conclusions from microscopy images.
