# Cellpose Plus

## Overview
Cellpose Plus is an advanced image processing framework designed for cell segmentation and image denoising. It leverages deep learning techniques to process both 2D and 3D images efficiently, allowing users to extract meaningful segmentation results from complex biological images. The framework supports various functionalities, including model training, evaluation, and data augmentation, while integrating GPU acceleration for faster computations.

## Purpose
The primary purpose of Cellpose Plus is to provide researchers and practitioners in the field of microscopy and cellular biology with a robust tool for accurately segmenting and analyzing cell images. By offering multiple model architectures, customizable training options, and extensive preprocessing capabilities, the framework aims to enhance the reliability and quality of image analysis results, ultimately facilitating research and discovery in biological sciences.
